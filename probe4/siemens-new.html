Applications to Siemens Scholarship Program 2026-27 are now closed.

Empower the future with Siemens Scholarship Program

Many engineering graduates face challenges due to limited industry exposure, skills gaps, and financial barriers. A more inclusive and industry-aligned education system can prepare students for better successful careers.

The Siemens Scholarship has grown proudly nurturing over 2300 students from 28 states. We support economically disadvantaged engineering students through four-year scholarships and holistic development based on the German dual education system to make them industry ready.

Creating Opportunities Nationwide

We are a Pan-India program supporting over 2,000 students across 28 states from Government Colleges.

Shaping Industry-Ready Youth

The program focuses on essential skills for engineers through foundational skills, business fundamentals and work-based learning.

Championing equal opportunity

We offer equal opportunity scholarships with 50% reserved for girls.

Highlights of the program

Key highlights of the Siemens Scholarship Program include:

  • Annual scholarship comprising reimbursement of tuition fees and educational allowances for books, hostel and additional classes
  • Holistic development program focusing on technical and functional training, soft skills enhancement, mentoring and real-world experience through internships and Siemens Mechatronics System Certiﬁcation Program (SMSCP)
  • Yearly scholarship renewal subject to students clearing all subjects and scoring first class*

*Terms & Conditions apply

Frequently asked questions

The Siemens Scholarship Program is for meritorious first-year engineering students studying at Government Colleges who are committed to academic excellence and building a successful career.

Eligibility requirements include:

  • First-year students of Government Engineering college (excluding IIT) from the following streams: Mechanical/Production, Electrical, Electronics, Electronics & Telecommunication, Computer Science/Information Technology and Instrumentation
  • Age: Up to 20 years
  • SSC: Minimum 60% aggregate
  • HSC or equivalent result: Minimum 50% aggregate and minimum 60% PCM aggregate
  • Annual family income: Not more than Rs. 2 lakhs
  • Eligibility is limited to one student per family
