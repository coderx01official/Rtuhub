Higher & Technical Education Rajasthan

Vision: The Government of Rajasthan aims to improve the quality of higher education, encourage creative research and bring positive change in various fields of higher education.

Objectives: Overseeing, monitoring, and regulating higher education programs in state colleges affiliated with various universities in benefit of students.

List of Departments and their functions

The Department of Higher Education is tasked by the Government of Rajasthan with implementing policy aspects, overseeing and funding the higher education system of the state, which includes universities, affiliated colleges and institutes. The Government of Rajasthan aims to build capacity, promote research and establish industry relationships for job opportunity to students.
